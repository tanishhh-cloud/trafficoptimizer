System Architecture

How Smart Adaptive Signals Work

Conventional traffic signals use pre-programmed fixed timers regardless of traffic density. The BYTEBUSTERS Adaptive System senses real-time lane demand to minimize delays.

1

COUNT

Sensors detect vehicles waiting on each approach road within 145 pixels of the stop line.

2

DECIDE

The smart controller dynamically calculates optimal green light duration (8s min to 28s max) based on queue size.

3

SWITCH

Signals transition safely through 2s yellow light and 1s all-red intervals to clear the intersection.

4

COMPARE

Both simulations share the identical deterministic arrival schedule for a scientifically fair performance comparison.

🎯 Project Purpose

Traffic congestion in urban centers causes severe delays, fuel waste, and carbon emissions. Adaptive traffic signals dynamically sense congestion and allocate green light time where demand is highest, reducing average delays by up to 30-50% without physical infrastructure changes.
